Biography

Dr. Uduak George is an accomplished scholar, researcher and academic administrator with extensive experience in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. He has contributed significantly to teaching, research, curriculum development and academic leadership within the University of Uyo and the wider academic community. His passion for innovation and student mentorship has helped produce graduates who excel in academia, industry and entrepreneurship. He actively collaborates with national and international research institutions, supervising undergraduate and postgraduate research projects while promoting excellence in teaching and community engagement.
