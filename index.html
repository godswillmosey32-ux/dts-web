Department Overview

The Department of Data Science is dedicated to excellence in teaching, research and innovation. Our programmes combine theoretical foundations with hands-on practical experience in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Mining, Business Intelligence, Data Engineering and Statistical Computing. Students graduate with the knowledge and practical skills required to solve complex real-world problems using data.
