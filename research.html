Research & Innovation

The Department of Data Science conducts innovative research in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Big Data, Cloud Computing and emerging technologies aimed at solving real-world problems.

Research Overview

The Department of Data Science promotes cutting-edge research through interdisciplinary collaboration, industry partnerships and innovative solutions driven by data and technology.

Our researchers explore advanced computational methods, intelligent systems, predictive analytics, digital transformation and applications of data science across different sectors including healthcare, education, finance, agriculture and industry.
