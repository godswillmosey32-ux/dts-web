General Questions

What is the Department of Data Science?

The department focuses on data analysis, artificial intelligence, machine learning and technology-driven solutions.

Where is the department located?

The Department of Data Science is located at the Faculty of Computing, University of Uyo.

What programmes does the department offer?

The department offers programmes focused on data science, computing, analytics and emerging technologies.

Admission FAQs

What are the admission requirements for Data Science?

Applicants must meet the university admission requirements and possess the required qualifications for the Data Science programme.

How can I apply for admission?

Applications are submitted through the approved University of Uyo admission process and official admission portal.

Can students from other science backgrounds apply?

Yes. Students with relevant science, technology and mathematics backgrounds may apply if they meet the requirements.

Is there a postgraduate programme?

Information about postgraduate programmes is provided by the department and university graduate school.

Academic FAQs

What courses are studied in Data Science?

Students study programming, statistics, databases, machine learning, artificial intelligence, analytics and data visualization.

How long is the Data Science programme?

The programme duration follows the approved university academic structure and regulations.

How are examinations conducted?

Examinations are conducted according to university academic policies and departmental guidelines.

Where can students access course materials?

Students can access learning materials through the student portal and departmental download resources.

Student Portal FAQs

How do I access the student portal?

Students can access the portal using their registered student login details.

What services are available on the student portal?

The portal provides access to course registration, academic records, announcements, resources and student support services.

I forgot my portal password. What should I do?

Contact the department support office or portal administrator for password recovery assistance.

Can I access the portal on mobile devices?

Yes. The portal is designed to work on computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Course Registration FAQs

When does course registration begin?

Course registration dates are announced through official departmental and university channels.

Can I edit my registered courses?

Course changes may be allowed within the approved registration period based on university regulations.

What happens if I miss course registration?

Students should contact the appropriate academic office immediately for guidance.

Where can I download registration forms?

Registration forms and related documents are available through the departmental downloads page.

Research FAQs

What research areas are available in Data Science?

Research areas include artificial intelligence, machine learning, big data analytics, data mining, cybersecurity and intelligent systems.

How can students participate in research projects?

Students can participate by working with lecturers, joining research groups and contributing to departmental projects.

Where can I find research guidelines?

Research guidelines, templates and academic resources are available on the department downloads page.

Research Resources

Can students publish research papers?

Yes. Students can publish research with proper guidance from supervisors and research coordinators.

SIWES & Internship FAQs

What is SIWES?

SIWES is an industrial training programme that allows students to gain practical experience in their field.

Who is eligible for SIWES?

Students who meet the approved academic requirements for industrial training are eligible to participate.

How do I register for internship placement?

Students should complete the required forms and follow the department's internship registration process.

Where do I submit my SIWES report?

SIWES reports are submitted according to departmental guidelines and deadlines.

Career FAQs

What career opportunities are available for Data Science graduates?

Graduates can work as data scientists, data analysts, machine learning engineers, software developers, researchers and technology consultants.

Does the department provide career support?

Yes. Career support includes mentorship, internship opportunities, professional guidance and industry connections.

Career Centre

Can students get help preparing CVs and interviews?

Students can access career resources and professional development support.

Are there opportunities for professional certifications?

Students are encouraged to pursue relevant technology certifications that improve their professional skills.

Alumni FAQs

How can graduates join the alumni network?

Graduates can register through the alumni portal and create their alumni profile.

Join Alumni

What benefits do alumni receive?

Alumni can participate in networking events, mentorship programmes, career opportunities and departmental activities.

Can alumni support current students?

Yes. Alumni can support students through mentorship, scholarships, career guidance and knowledge sharing.

How can alumni update their information?

Alumni can update their profiles through the alumni registration platform.
