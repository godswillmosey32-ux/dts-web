Laboratory Safety Guidelines

  • Students must obtain authorization before using laboratory equipment.
  • Follow all instructions provided by laboratory supervisors.
  • Handle computing devices and electronic equipment carefully.
  • Food and drinks are not allowed inside laboratories.
  • Report damaged equipment immediately.
  • Maintain a clean and organized working environment.
  • Follow cybersecurity and data protection policies.

Laboratory Policies

Equipment Usage Policy

All laboratory equipment must be used responsibly and only for approved academic, teaching and research activities.

  • Users must log equipment usage.
  • Unauthorized modification of equipment is prohibited.
  • Damaged equipment must be reported immediately.

Research Ethics Policy

All research activities conducted within the laboratories must follow ethical standards and institutional guidelines.

  • Respect data privacy.
  • Maintain research integrity.
  • Properly acknowledge research contributions.

Access Policy

Laboratory access is controlled to ensure safety, security and effective use of resources.

  • Valid identification required.
  • Supervisor approval may be required.
  • Visitors must be accompanied.
