Who We Are

About the Department

The Department of Data Science at the University of Uyo is committed to academic excellence, innovative research, industry collaboration and the development of globally competitive graduates equipped with modern analytical, computational and artificial intelligence skills.

Our Story

Department History

The Department of Data Science was established to address the growing demand for professionals capable of extracting valuable insights from data using modern computational techniques.

Since its inception, the department has remained committed to excellence in teaching, research, innovation and community service.

Through strategic collaborations with industry and academic partners, the department has become one of the leading centres for Data Science education and research in Nigeria.

Established

Founding Year

Established in 2022

The Department of Data Science was officially established under the Faculty of Computing in 2022 to provide quality education and research in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, Data Engineering, Business Intelligence and related fields.

Since its establishment, the department has continued to expand its academic programmes, research activities and international collaborations.

Our Journey

Growth Timeline

2022 · Department Established

The Department of Data Science was inaugurated under the Faculty of Computing with its first cohort of undergraduate students and a vision to become a centre of excellence in Data Science education and research.

2023 · Academic Expansion

New laboratories were introduced, additional academic staff were recruited, and industry partnerships were established to support practical training and internship opportunities.

2024 · Research Development

The department launched several research groups in Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, Machine Learning and Cloud Computing, resulting in increased publications and conference participation.

2025 · Innovation & Collaboration

International collaborations with universities and technology companies strengthened research activities while students won several national innovation competitions.

2026 · Continued Excellence

The department continues to expand postgraduate programmes, modernize laboratories and promote interdisciplinary research that addresses national and global challenges.

Our Thinking

Department Philosophy

The Department of Data Science believes that data is a vital resource for innovation, scientific discovery and sustainable development.

Our philosophy is to provide students with a balanced education that combines strong theoretical foundations, practical laboratory experience, ethical responsibility, critical thinking and continuous innovation.

We are committed to developing graduates who can solve real-world challenges through data-driven decision-making, artificial intelligence and advanced computational methods.
