Registration Guide

Step 1: Departmental Clearance

New and returning students should complete all required clearance procedures before proceeding with registration.

Step 2: School Fees Payment

Pay approved school fees through the University's official payment platform and keep your payment receipt for reference.

Step 3: Course Registration

Register all approved courses online after consulting your Academic Adviser.

Step 4: Print Registration Slip

Print and submit all registration documents to the Department for verification and record keeping.

Course Registration

Registration Procedure

  • Login to the Student Portal.
  • Select the current academic session.
  • Choose approved courses.
  • Submit for Academic Adviser approval.
  • Print the final course registration form.

Important Guidelines

  • Register only approved courses.
  • Observe minimum and maximum credit units.
  • Meet registration deadlines.
  • Late registration may attract penalties.

Examination Information

Examination Rules

  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before the examination.
  • No mobile phones or unauthorized materials.
  • Follow all examination instructions.

SIWES / Industrial Training

Eligibility

Students who have reached the approved level are eligible to participate in the Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES).

Duration

Industrial Training usually lasts between three and six months depending on the University's academic regulations.

Placement Process

  • Obtain SIWES approval.
  • Secure an industrial placement.
  • Receive departmental approval.
  • Begin industrial training.

Assessment

  • Maintain a SIWES Logbook.
  • Submit a final report.
  • Attend oral presentation.
  • Complete departmental evaluation.
