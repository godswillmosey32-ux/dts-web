Welcome to Admissions

Why Choose Our Department?

The Department of Data Science provides world-class teaching, modern laboratories, experienced lecturers, industry-focused training and opportunities for cutting-edge research. Graduates are equipped with the knowledge and practical skills needed for successful careers in technology, finance, healthcare, government and research.

Admission Cycle

Admissions are offered annually for undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. Applicants are encouraged to submit complete applications before the official closing date and regularly monitor the University portal for updates on screening, admission lists and registration.

General Admission Overview

Who Can Apply?

  • UTME Candidates
  • Direct Entry Candidates
  • Transfer Students
  • International Applicants
  • Postgraduate Applicants

Admission Principles

Admission is based on merit, eligibility, and compliance with the admission requirements of the University of Uyo and the Department of Data Science.

General Admission Requirements

Eligibility

Applicants seeking admission into the Department of Data Science must satisfy both the general admission requirements of the University of Uyo and the specific departmental requirements.

  • Must possess the required academic qualifications.
  • Must meet the approved departmental cut-off mark.
  • Must successfully participate in the Post-UTME screening (where applicable).
  • Must provide authentic academic credentials.

Important Notice

Admission is offered strictly on merit and in accordance with the admission guidelines approved by the University Senate and the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB). Applicants are advised to regularly check the University's official admission portal for updates.

UTME (JAMB) Admission Requirements

UTME Requirements

  • Choose the University of Uyo as First Choice Institution.
  • Obtain the minimum JAMB score approved for the admission year.
  • Participate in the University's Post-UTME screening exercise.
  • Upload O'Level results to the JAMB CAPS portal.

Recommended JAMB Subject Combination

  • English Language (Compulsory)
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry or Biology

O'Level Requirements

Required Subjects

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • One other Science subject

Accepted Examination Bodies

  • WAEC
  • NECO
  • NABTEB (subject to University regulations)

Candidates may combine results in accordance with the University's admission policy.

Direct Entry Admission

Eligible Qualifications

  • National Diploma (ND)
  • Higher National Diploma (HND)
  • NCE
  • IJMB
  • JUPEB
  • GCE A-Level

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification from a recognized institution.
  • Good academic performance in the previous programme.
  • Meet all University of Uyo Direct Entry requirements.
  • Submit all supporting documents during screening.

Admission Level

Successful Direct Entry candidates are normally admitted into 200 Level, subject to University regulations and departmental approval.

Admission Checklist

  • ✔ Complete the online application form.
  • ✔ Upload all required credentials.
  • ✔ Verify your JAMB and O'Level details.
  • ✔ Print your application acknowledgement slip.
  • ✔ Monitor the admission portal for screening and admission updates.
  • ✔ Accept your admission on JAMB CAPS after receiving an offer.

Transfer Students

Eligibility

Students from recognized universities may apply for transfer admission into the Department of Data Science subject to the University of Uyo transfer policy.

  • Must be in good academic standing.
  • Must have completed at least one academic session.
  • Must satisfy departmental admission requirements.
  • Transfer is subject to available space.

Required Documents

  • Academic Transcript
  • Letter of Good Standing
  • Admission Letter from Previous Institution
  • O'Level Result
  • Birth Certificate
  • Completed Transfer Application Form

International Students

Admission Requirements

  • Equivalent secondary school qualification.
  • Official academic transcripts.
  • International passport.
  • English language proficiency (where applicable).
  • Valid study visa before resumption.

Support Services

International students receive support with admissions, orientation, accommodation guidance, immigration procedures and academic advising throughout their studies.

Application Process

Step 1

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I gain admission?

Apply through JAMB (for UTME candidates) or Direct Entry, meet the required cut-off mark, participate in the screening exercise, and satisfy all departmental requirements.

What are the O'Level requirements?

Five (5) credit passes including English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and one other relevant science subject.

Can I combine WAEC and NECO results?

Yes, subject to the admission policy of the University of Uyo for the current admission session.

Is Direct Entry available?

Yes. Qualified candidates with ND, HND, NCE, IJMB, JUPEB or equivalent qualifications may apply.

How long is the B.Sc. programme?

The programme lasts four (4) years for UTME candidates and three (3) years for Direct Entry candidates.
