Course Overview

The Department of Data Science provides a comprehensive curriculum designed to develop expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Programming, Statistics, Database Systems, Cloud Computing, Big Data Technologies and Data Engineering.

Each course combines theoretical knowledge with practical applications through lectures, projects, laboratory sessions and industry-focused training.

Students are equipped with the skills required to solve real-world problems using modern data-driven technologies.

Course Advising

Academic Adviser Guidance

Students are advised to meet their assigned Academic Adviser before course registration to ensure correct course selection and academic progression.

Credit Unit Requirements

Students must register approved courses within the minimum and maximum credit unit limits specified by University regulations.

Course Selection Guidelines

  • Select only courses approved for your level.
  • Complete prerequisite courses before advanced courses.
  • Seek approval before adding or dropping courses.
  • Maintain good academic standing.
