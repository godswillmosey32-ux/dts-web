Academic Programmes

The Department of Data Science offers high-quality undergraduate and postgraduate programmes designed to equip students with advanced knowledge, practical skills and research experience in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics and related fields.

Programme Overview

Undergraduate Programme

The Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Data Science provides a strong foundation in mathematics, statistics, computing, programming, databases, artificial intelligence and data analytics. Students gain practical experience through laboratory work, industry internships and research projects.

Explore B.Sc.

Postgraduate Programmes

The department offers PGD, M.Sc. and Ph.D. programmes that prepare graduates for advanced research, academic careers, industry leadership and innovation in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

Explore Postgraduate

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Data Science

Programme Overview

The Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Data Science is a comprehensive undergraduate programme designed to equip students with theoretical knowledge and practical skills in data analysis, programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, database systems, cloud computing and business analytics. Students learn to collect, process, analyse and visualize large datasets to support informed decision-making across different sectors.

Admission Requirements

UTME (JAMB)

  • Minimum UTME score as approved by the University.
  • Five (5) O'Level credit passes including:
  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • One other Science subject.

Direct Entry

  • OND, HND or NCE in relevant disciplines.
  • A-Level passes in Mathematics and related subjects.
  • IJMB or JUPEB qualifications accepted according to University regulations.

Programme Duration

UTME Candidates

Four (4) academic sessions consisting of eight (8) semesters.

Direct Entry Candidates

Three (3) academic sessions consisting of six (6) semesters.

Graduation Requirements

  • Successfully complete all compulsory and elective courses.
  • Earn the minimum required credit units approved by the University.
  • Complete and defend the final-year research project.
  • Successfully complete SIWES/Industrial Training.
  • Meet the University's minimum CGPA requirement for graduation.
  • Fulfil all departmental and university academic regulations.

Postgraduate Programmes

The Department of Data Science offers postgraduate programmes that develop advanced knowledge, research skills and professional competence in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and related computing disciplines.

Postgraduate Diploma (PGD)

Programme Overview

The PGD programme is designed for graduates who wish to acquire professional knowledge and practical skills in Data Science or transition into the field from related disciplines.

Entry Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognized university.
  • HND (Upper Credit) in a related discipline may be considered.
  • Applicants must satisfy the University's postgraduate admission requirements.

Duration

Full-Time: 2 Semesters (1 Academic Session)

Expected Outcomes

Graduates acquire practical knowledge in programming, database systems, statistics, data analysis and machine learning.

Master of Science (M.Sc.)

Programme Overview

The M.Sc. programme prepares students for advanced professional practice, research and doctoral studies through coursework, seminars and a supervised research dissertation.

Entry Requirements

  • B.Sc. in Data Science, Computer Science or related field.
  • Minimum Second Class Honours (Lower Division).
  • Applicants must meet the School of Postgraduate Studies requirements.

Duration

Full-Time: 3–4 Semesters

Part-Time: 4–6 Semesters

Research Areas

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning
  • Data Mining
  • Big Data Analytics
  • Cloud Computing
  • Business Intelligence

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)

Programme Overview

The Ph.D. programme is research-intensive and prepares scholars for careers in academia, industry and research institutions through original contributions to knowledge.

Entry Requirements

  • M.Sc. in Data Science or a closely related discipline.
  • Minimum CGPA as prescribed by the University.
  • Submission of a research proposal may be required.

Duration

Full-Time: 6–8 Semesters

Part-Time: As approved by the School of Postgraduate Studies.

Research Focus

  • Deep Learning
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision
  • Cybersecurity Analytics
  • Health Informatics
  • Financial Data Analytics
